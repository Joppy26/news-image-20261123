## The day's stories

- **Ukraine:** a Russian drone hit a block of flats in the Kyiv region, leaving 14 people missing; a Russian cargo ship was still burning off Finland after a suspected Ukrainian drone strike.
- **Climate:** COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, ended with no overarching deal after the final text dropped any mention of fossil fuels.
- **Indonesia:** a fire at a candy factory in Banten killed at least 3 workers, with dozens still missing as the search resumed.
- **The Gulf:** Iran's Revolutionary Guard claimed missile and drone strikes on US Patriot radars at bases in Bahrain; a Houthi missile aimed at western Saudi Arabia was intercepted. The radar claim is Iran's account and has not been independently verified; Bahrain confirmed a fire at a civilian industrial site with no injuries.

Claims are shown as claims, and casualty figures are those reported at the time.
